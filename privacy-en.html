Touch Time · Privacy Policy

App: Touch Time (HarmonyOS NEXT) · Developer: Ye Zhenxian (individual developer) · Effective: October 22, 2026 · Version: V1.1 (English translation; the Chinese text prevails)

1. Introduction

This policy applies to the "Touch Time" app (the "App"). We understand the importance of your personal information and will protect it in accordance with the Personal Information Protection Law of the People's Republic of China and the rules of the Huawei platform. Please read this policy carefully before using the App.

2. How We Collect and Use Information

2.1 Account information (when you sign in with a HUAWEI ID)

With your authorization, we obtain account identifiers (OpenID, UnionID) through the HUAWEI ID service to identify your account, keep you signed in, and associate your app data. We never receive your HUAWEI ID password, and we do not obtain your phone number or other extra information unless you separately authorize it. Avatar and nickname are read only within the scope you authorize when you actively use the relevant display feature.

2.2 App usage data (moments, check-ins, etc.)

Data you create is stored on your device first. In mainland China, when the system version, Cloud version, sign-in state and the Cloud sync switch all meet the required conditions, the App can sync the following six categories of account data across devices signed in with the same account through HUAWEI Cloud: moments, categories, player progress and achievements, pet preferences, widget entitlements, and the time-boundary ledger. Sync is used for cross-device restore and consistency, and does not include device-level settings, sign-in credentials, privacy consent records or product-improvement analytics.

2.3 Product improvement analytics (HUAWEI Analytics)

To improve the product and its stability, the App records usage statistics (for example app launches, page views, feature toggles, membership purchase flow results, errors and durations) and uploads them to Huawei's analytics service through HUAWEI Analytics (Analytics Kit). Analytics content is limited to event names plus a small number of enumerated or numeric parameters (such as app version, language, page name, event name, duration in milliseconds, membership state and error category).

We never upload content you enter: moment titles, notes and diary text you type are not included in analytics and are never used as analytics parameters. Analytics contains no name, phone number, email address or HUAWEI ID password.

Before you accept this policy, the App does not initialize the analytics service and sends nothing. After you withdraw consent, analytics stops immediately and any queued events that have not been sent are cleared.

2.4 Payment information (for paid services)

Membership and theme purchases are completed through HUAWEI In-App Purchases and processed by Huawei. We do not collect or store bank card numbers or payment passwords; we only receive the payment result in order to unlock the corresponding entitlement.

3. Permissions

Permissions currently used: network access (for HUAWEI ID sign-in, order status queries and product-improvement analytics upload). The App does not request contacts, location, camera, microphone or photo library permissions. If a new permission is added in future, we will explain it and obtain your consent before use.

4. Storage and Protection

Your app data is submitted and stored on your device first. When the conditions above are met and you enable sync, the six categories of account data are stored in your HUAWEI Cloud through the system's cloud data sync capability. We rely on the data isolation, encrypted storage and account isolation mechanisms provided by the system to prevent unauthorized access, disclosure or destruction. A successful local save does not mean the data has already been uploaded; the App still works when the network is unavailable or sync is off. Note that uninstalling the App or clearing app data deletes local data, and data that has not been synced may not be recoverable.

Product-improvement analytics events uploaded through HUAWEI Analytics are stored and deleted by Huawei under its own rules; see section 5.1 for storage location and retention.

5. Third-Party SDKs and Sharing, Transfer and Public Disclosure

5.1 Third-party SDKs we integrate

The App currently integrates only the SDK listed below; it contains no third-party advertising, push or crash-monitoring SDK.

  • SDK name: HUAWEI Analytics SDK (Analytics Kit, HarmonyOS edition)
  • SDK provider: Huawei Software Technologies Co., Ltd.
  • Categories of personal data collected: app information (package name, app version, SDK name and version); device information (device model, OS and version, screen width and height, system language, memory and storage overview, remaining battery and charging status, AAID advertising identifier); network information (IP address used to determine country/region, network type); app usage information (automatically collected app launch/update/page events, plus the custom events and parameters reported by the App)
  • Purpose: to provide data collection, statistics and analysis services for the App (new/active/retention, event analysis, version and error analysis) in order to improve the product experience and stability
  • Privacy statement link: HUAWEI Analytics SDK Privacy Statement

Huawei processes this data as an entrusted processor under the SDK Privacy Statement and the HUAWEI AppGallery Connect Privacy and Security Statement: we are the personal information processor and Huawei is the entrusted processor. Storage location and retention follow the "Project access settings" of this app in the AGC console: currently configured to process data within mainland China, with a 14-month retention period, after which HUAWEI Analytics deletes it automatically.

5.2 Sharing, transfer and public disclosure

  • We do not sell your personal information to third parties;
  • We do not share your information with third parties except: (1) interactions with Huawei that are necessary for HUAWEI ID sign-in, HUAWEI In-App Purchases, the HUAWEI Cloud sync you enable, and product-improvement analytics; (2) disclosure required by laws, regulations or competent authorities;
  • The App contains no third-party advertising SDK and does not use your information for advertising tracking; analytics excludes content you enter and is not used for ad targeting.

6. Your Rights

  • Access and correction: you can view and modify your data directly in the App;
  • Deletion: you can delete local data with the in-app data clearing feature or by uninstalling the App. You can also turn off sync for this app in "Settings - HUAWEI ID - Cloud", or use the "Stop syncing and delete cloud data" entry to request deletion of the cloud copy. Turning off sync or deleting the cloud copy does not delete local data; when offline, the deletion request waits for the next sync and does not complete immediately;
  • Account cancellation: you can apply through the in-app entry or the contact details in section 9. After verifying your identity we will delete data associated with the account (except where laws require retention);
  • Withdrawing consent: you can withdraw consent in "About & privacy" in the App, or by signing out, turning off Cloud sync, or stopping use of the App. After withdrawal, product-improvement analytics and account-related capabilities stop immediately and queued unsent events are cleared. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before it.

7. Minors

If you are a minor, please read this policy under the guidance of a guardian and use the App only with the guardian's consent. We do not actively collect personal information from minors. If we learn that we have collected such information without verifiable guardian consent, we will delete it as soon as possible.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may revise this policy from time to time. Updates will be announced prominently in the App or on this page; for material changes we will ask for your consent again.

9. Contact Us

For any question, comment or complaint about this policy or personal information protection, contact us at yzx4036@gmail.com. We will reply within 15 business days.
